How Aggregation Works - Honest Model

We don't own 99MW. We have tie-up with generators who own it. We aggregate 5-10 factories to make 2-3MW group, then sign PPA with generator on your behalf via Group Captive SPV. You get same power, we get facilitation fee - transparent.

Step 2: SPV with 26% equity
