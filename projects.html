How Aggregation Works - Honest Model

CKR PowerEdge acts as an advisor and facilitator, not as the owner of the generation assets shown. For each enquiry, we work to identify a suitable project or partner, examine the proposed commercial structure and explain the documents required. Whether aggregation or a group-captive structure is practical depends on the consumers, project, state rules and written agreements.

Step 1: Confirm participating consumers and load
Step 2: Verify ownership and consumption tests
Step 3: Compare verified landed costs
Documents to verify for each project:
  • Generator PPA copy (with tariff)
  • SPV share certificate (26% proof)
  • APTRANSCO Open Access NOC
  • Monthly generation & adjustment report
