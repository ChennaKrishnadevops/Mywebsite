Green Energy Open Access in Andhra Pradesh

As per APERC Green Energy Open Access, Charges and Banking Regulations 2023. Compliant & Transparent.

Green Open Access allows HT commercial & industrial consumers (≥100kW) to purchase renewable power directly from wind-solar generators via open access through APTRANSCO network.

Quick Eligibility Check
✅ HT Connection ≥100kW
✅ 11kV and above voltage
✅ No outstanding DISCOM dues
✅ Meter with ABT / TOD facility
✅ Located in APSPDCL / APEPDCL area

Exact charges vary by DISCOM circle (Anantapur, Kurnool) and calculated in your free audit. Below is indicative.

Charge ComponentApprox Range /unit

⚠️ Important: Group Captive model (26% equity) gets CSS waiver benefit as per rules. Third party has full CSS. Your final landed cost = Generator Tariff (₹3.8-4.2) + Above Charges. Typically ₹5.2-6.0 vs DISCOM HT ₹7.8-8.5.

Banking Facility in AP

Excess wind-solar generated in day/windy season can be banked with DISCOM and adjusted at night/low wind months. As per APERC 2023, banking allowed with 8% banking charge. We do monthly energy adjustment & settlement.

Types of Green Open Access

1. Third Party Open Access
You buy power from generator via PPA, no equity needed. Quickest. CSS applicable.
2. Group Captive (Most Savings)
You invest 26% equity in SPV power company + consume 51% of its power. Lowest charges, long-term ownership benefit. Needs SPV formation.
3. Captive (For single large consumer >5MW)
Full 100% ownership of plant. Not for aggregated small factories.

Documents Required for Open Access NOC

  • Last 12 months HT bills (APSPDCL)
  • Single Line Diagram + Connected Load
  • Company PAN, GST, Incorporation
  • Board Resolution for Open Access
  • Undertaking for 26% equity (if Group Captive)
