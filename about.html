I am P. ChennaKrishna Reddy, an M.Tech Power Electronics professional based in Tadipatri, Andhra Pradesh. I help commercial and industrial consumers assess renewable electricity procurement, open access and group-captive options, with India-wide enquiries welcome.

My approach starts with the factory's interval load, electricity bills and operating priorities. I explain the assumptions behind landed-cost estimates, compare suitable procurement routes and coordinate with relevant project partners. Regulations, charges, counterparties and approvals differ by state, so each opportunity needs a location-specific review.

What I Do - Facilitator, Not Big Corporate

  • Review C&I bills, load profiles and renewable procurement options for each site
  • Explain third-party open access and group-captive structures, including their obligations
  • Coordinate documentation and feasibility enquiries with the relevant state agencies, DISCOMs and project partners
  • Provide a no-obligation preliminary power-cost assessment; scope and timing are confirmed for each enquiry

My Mission

To help Indian businesses make informed, transparent decisions about renewable power. I aim to explain the numbers clearly, disclose project relationships and never present estimates, partner capacity or potential savings as guaranteed outcomes.
